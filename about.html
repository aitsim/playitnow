About PlayItNow

Free, original browser games that load fast and play instantly on any device.

What is PlayItNow?

PlayItNow is an independent browser gaming site started in 2026. The idea is simple: you open a link and you're playing within seconds — no downloads, no accounts, and nothing to install. Everything here runs directly in your web browser, so it works the same on a laptop, a phone, a tablet, or a school Chromebook.

Built in-house

Every game on PlayItNow is designed and built by us rather than pulled from a giant catalog, including the code, the artwork, and the sound effects. That keeps the library small and curated, and it means we can keep improving each game after it launches.

What you can play

  • Arcade & action — fast, reflex-driven games like Neon Slither, Neon Blob, Neon Onslaught, Neon Stack, Neon Dino, and Neon Climb.
  • Puzzle — Neon Pop, a bubble shooter.
  • Shop & character games — Buzzy Bear's Honey Shop and Penguin Fish Market, where you gather, stock, and sell to customers.
  • Coloring & creativity — Mini Art Studio, a relaxing digital coloring book.
  • Word games — Hangman, the classic word-guessing game.

How the site is funded

PlayItNow is free to play. The site is supported by advertising, including Google AdSense, which helps cover hosting and lets us keep building new games. You can read how ads and cookies work on our Privacy Policy page.
