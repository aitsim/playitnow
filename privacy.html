2. Collecting and using your data

Personal data

PlayItNow does not require an account or sign-up to play any game on the site. The only Personal Data we ask for is what you choose to give us if you use the contact form:

  • Email address
  • Name
  • Whatever you write in the message field

We use this only to respond to you — never for unrelated marketing.

Usage data

Usage Data is collected automatically when you use the Service. This may include your device's IP address, browser type and version, the pages you visit, the time and date of your visit, time spent on those pages, and other diagnostic data. If you access the Service from a mobile device, we may also automatically collect the type of device you use, a unique device identifier, and your mobile operating system.

Tracking technologies and cookies

We — and the advertising and analytics partners we work with — use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate the Service, remember your preferences, and serve relevant ads. You can instruct your browser to refuse all Cookies, but some parts of the Service may not work as intended if you do.

  • Essential cookies (session) — needed for core site functionality.
  • Cookie-notice cookies (persistent) — remember that you've seen our cookie notice, if shown.
  • Functionality cookies (persistent) — remember preferences like display settings, so you don't have to re-set them on every visit.
  • Advertising cookies (third-party) — set by our ad partners, such as Google AdSense, to serve and measure ads. See Section 4 below for details and opt-out links.

How we use your data

The Company may use Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Service, including monitoring how it's used so we can keep it fast and reliable.
  • To respond to you when you contact us through the contact form or by email.
  • For business transfers — if PlayItNow is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your data may be part of the assets transferred, subject to the commitments in this Policy.
  • For other purposes, such as data analysis, identifying usage trends, and evaluating and improving the Service.

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Service, analyze usage, or serve advertising — for example, Google AdSense and web analytics tools.
  • A successor company, in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.
  • Law enforcement or regulators, where required by law (see Section 7).
  • Anyone else, with your consent, for any purpose you've agreed to.

Data retention

We keep Personal Data only as long as necessary for the purposes in this Policy — for example, contact form messages are kept only long enough to resolve your inquiry, unless we're legally required to retain them longer. Usage Data is generally retained for shorter periods, except where it's needed for security or to improve the Service.

International transfer

Your information may be processed by service providers located outside your state, province, country, or other governmental jurisdiction, where data protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction. By using the Service, you consent to that transfer. We take reasonable steps to ensure your data is handled securely wherever it's processed.

Your rights

Since PlayItNow doesn't use accounts, there's no dashboard to manage — but you can still ask us to access, correct, or delete any Personal Data you've given us (for example, through the contact form) by reaching out to us directly. We may need to retain certain information where we have a legal obligation to do so.

We may disclose Personal Data in the good-faith belief that it's necessary to:

Security

We care about the security of your data, but no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we use commercially reasonable measures to protect your information, we can't guarantee absolute security.
