How to play Mini Art Studio

Mini Art Studio is a simple, relaxing coloring book — there's no score, no timer, and no way to lose. Pick a color from the palette along the bottom (or tap the rainbow swatch for a full custom color picker), then tap or click anywhere inside the picture to fill that shape with color using the paint bucket tool. Switch to the brush to draw freehand in any size, or the eraser to remove color, both with three adjustable brush sizes. Made a mistake? Undo and redo step through your changes, and Clear wipes the picture back to a blank outline if you want a fresh start. Use the arrows on either side of the canvas to move between pictures — a kitten, puppy, horse, butterfly, shark, and dinosaur to start, with your coloring on each one kept as you switch back and forth during your visit.

Tips & strategies

  • Start with the paint bucket. It's the fastest way to get a full picture colored in — fill the big shapes first, then switch to the brush for small details.
  • Use the brush for shading and details. Try a small brush size for fine touches like adding extra patterns inside a shape you've already filled.
  • Don't worry about mistakes. Undo steps back through your last several changes, so there's no pressure to get every fill perfect the first time.
  • Try the custom color picker. The rainbow swatch opens a full color picker if you want a shade that isn't in the main palette.
  • Your coloring stays put while you browse. Switching to another picture and back keeps your progress on both — no need to finish one before trying another.

Mini Art Studio — FAQ

How do I color a picture?

Pick a color from the palette, make sure the paint bucket tool is selected, then click or tap inside any shape to fill it with that color. Switch to the brush tool to draw freehand instead.

Can I use my own custom colors?

Yes — tap the rainbow swatch at the end of the color palette to open a full color picker and choose any shade you like.

What happens if I make a mistake?

Tap Undo to step back through your recent changes, or Redo to bring them back. Clear wipes the current picture back to a blank outline if you want to start over completely.

Is my coloring saved?

Each picture keeps its coloring while you switch between pictures during your visit. It isn't saved permanently between visits yet, so a page refresh will start you fresh.

How many pictures are there?

Six to start — a kitten, puppy, horse, butterfly, shark, and dinosaur — with more pictures and categories like trees, flowers, and everyday objects planned for the future.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes — every tool works with touch, dragging your finger to draw or erase and tapping to fill, the same as using a mouse on desktop.
