How to play Buzzy Bear's Honey Shop

Buzzy Bear's Honey Shop is a harvest-and-sell shop management game — part honey tycoon, part animal shop simulator — played one 90-second day at a time, no buttons for the core loop, just walk where you need to go. Walk Buzzy up to the honey tree and he automatically starts harvesting raw honeycomb — a progress ring fills above his head, and each piece pops out and stacks visibly on his back. Two angry bees patrol loose loops around the tree, and bumping into one knocks a piece of honeycomb right out of his paws, so time your approach. Once you're carrying honeycomb, simply walk it over to the honey stand — stocking happens automatically too, and jars pop into view on the shelf. Customers — a bunny and a fox — walk in, join a line of up to three, and buy a jar the moment you have stock; coins spill out near the register and it's up to you to walk over and collect them yourself. Spend coins any time on faster feet, a bigger basket, quicker harvesting, or a one-time Helper Bee hire that harvests and stocks honeycomb entirely on its own, plus a second honey hive that appears dimmed and locked on the map until you've saved up enough to unlock it just by walking up to it. Everything you buy sticks around for the next day too. When the timer hits zero, your day's results are tallied against your best day yet, and the next day starts right where you left off — same coins, same upgrades, slightly busier shop.

Tips & strategies

  • Bank a full basket before you sell. Walking back and forth wastes time — harvest up to your carry capacity, then make one trip to the stand.
  • Time your harvest trips around the bees. They loop around each tree on fixed patrol paths — approach from the side they've just moved away from rather than walking straight through them.
  • Don't leave coins sitting on the ground. Coins from a sale wait near the stand until you physically walk over them — swing by and collect before you head back to the trees.
  • Save toward the second hive. It's a bigger one-time cost than the small upgrades, but once unlocked it's a permanent second harvest spot with its own bee to dodge.
  • Buy Bigger Basket early. Carrying more honeycomb per trip cuts down on walking, and the upgrade carries into every future day too.
  • Keep some stock ready. Customers only wait a few seconds — an empty stand when they reach the front of the line means a missed sale.
  • Spend coins as you go. Upgrades help immediately and never expire, so there's rarely a reason to sit on a big coin balance.

Buzzy Bear's Honey Shop — FAQ

How do I play Buzzy Bear's Honey Shop?

Move Buzzy with WASD or the arrow keys. Just walk up to a tree or hive and he automatically starts harvesting honeycomb — no button needed. Walk it over to the stand and it stocks automatically too. Customers buy on their own once you're stocked, and coins appear near the register for you to walk over and collect.

What do the upgrades do, and do they carry over?

Speed increases your movement speed, Basket lets you carry more honeycomb per trip, and Harvest shortens how long each harvest takes. Each has five levels with rising costs, and yes — your levels and coins are saved and carry into every future day, they never reset.

How do I unlock the second hive?

You'll see a second, dimmed tree on the map with a sign showing its coin cost. Once you've saved up enough coins, just walk up to it — it unlocks automatically and becomes a second working harvest spot, complete with its own bee to watch out for.

What does the Helper Bee do?

It's a one-time hire from the upgrade panel. Once hired, a friendly bee (shown with a green tint, easy to tell apart from the angry hazard bees) automatically flies to the tree, harvests honeycomb, and carries it to the stand on its own — completely independent of what Buzzy is doing.

What happens if a bee touches me?

You'll drop one piece of carried honeycomb (if you're carrying any) and get knocked back a short distance. There's a brief invulnerability window afterward, so you won't get hit twice in a row.

What happens if a customer waits too long?

If the stand is empty when a customer reaches the front of the line, they'll wait a few seconds and then leave without buying, counted as a missed customer in your end-of-day results.

Is my progress saved?

Yes — your coins, upgrade levels, current day, and best day's earnings are all saved locally in your browser, so everything is right where you left it next time you visit on the same device and browser.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes — an on-screen directional pad appears automatically on touch devices for movement, the same as WASD or the arrow keys on desktop. Harvesting, stocking, and collecting coins are all automatic on both.

Is Buzzy Bear an original character?

Yes — Buzzy Bear and the Honey Shop theme were created in-house for PlayItNow. It's not based on or affiliated with any other game.

Is Buzzy Bear's Honey Shop like Monkey Mart?

It's in the same genre — a harvest-and-sell shop management game where you gather resources, stock a stand, serve customers, and spend earnings on upgrades — but with an entirely original bear-and-honey theme, cast, and art style rather than a copy. If you enjoy games like Monkey Mart or other shop simulator and tycoon-style games, the core loop will feel familiar while everything else is our own.
