Hangman instructions — how to play

The goal of Hangman is simple: guess the letters in the secret word before the stick figure is finished. Every round starts with a topic and a hidden word shown as a row of blank tiles — one tile per letter. Guess a letter by clicking or tapping it on the on-screen keyboard, or by typing it on your physical keyboard. If the letter is in the word, it appears in every spot where it belongs and you score 10 points. If it isn't, the stick figure by the wooden frame gets another piece: first the rope, then the head, body, both arms, and finally both legs. You can make six mistakes safely — the seventh wrong guess finishes the drawing and ends the round. But don't worry, this stick figure never gets hurt: if you run out of guesses he just bursts into tears, and you can try another word right away. Uncover every letter first and he cheers, you earn 2 diamonds, and you move up a level, where the words get a little longer.

Hangman tips & strategies

  • Start with the vowels. A, E, I, O and U appear in almost every word, and there are only five of them, so you'll usually land one within a couple of guesses. Even one vowel can point straight to the answer — a pattern like _ A _ A _ A practically spells BANANA.
  • Learn "ETAOIN SHRDLU". That odd-looking phrase lists the most common letters in English, roughly in order: E, T, A, O, I, N, S, H, R, D, L, U. After your vowels, work through these before you try anything rare.
  • Use the topic as a clue. If the topic is Kitchen Tools, think about what's actually in a kitchen before you guess — it narrows the possibilities a lot.
  • Watch the word length. A three-letter word and a ten-letter word call for very different guesses — short words often have just one vowel, and long words usually contain common endings like -ING or -ER.
  • Save J, Q, X and Z for later. These rare letters trip people up. Don't lead with them, but keep them in mind when the common letters have all missed.
  • Guess the whole word when you can. After a few letters are revealed, look at the gaps and try to picture the full word — then fill in the remaining letters with confidence.
  • Save your diamonds. A hint reveals one random missing letter for 1 diamond, and you only earn 2 per word — keep them for the tough ones.

What are the hardest Hangman words?

In general, the trickiest Hangman words are short ones packed with rare letters, like jazz, fizz or jinx — with so few letters and no common ones to find, there's very little to work with. Words with no regular vowels are just as awkward: rhythm, psych, synth and myth leave a vowel-first strategy with nothing to reveal. The words in this game are everyday vocabulary chosen by topic, so they're friendlier — but the same strategies apply, and the longer words at higher levels will still put your guessing skills to the test.

What do you learn from playing Hangman?

Hangman is a quiet way to practice spelling, vocabulary and pattern recognition. Every guess makes you think about which letters go together, how words are built, and what fits the topic — which is why it's a long-time favorite for classrooms and family game time. Playing by topic also helps expand your everyday knowledge: animals, foods, clothes, jobs, and a little mythology. If you like word puzzles such as word search, crosswords or cryptograms, Hangman is a friendly warm-up for the same kind of thinking.

Hangman — FAQ

How do I play Hangman?

Guess one letter at a time by tapping the on-screen keyboard or pressing keys on your own keyboard. Correct letters fill in the blanks; wrong ones add a piece to the stick figure. Reveal the whole word before all seven pieces are drawn.

How many wrong guesses do I get?

Seven in total. The pieces appear in order — rope, head, body, two arms, two legs — and the seventh wrong guess ends the round.

What happens when I lose a round?

Nothing scary — the stick figure just starts crying and the game shows you the word. You keep your level and your diamonds, and you can jump straight into another word.

What are diamonds for?

You start with 3, and earn 2 more each time you solve a word. Spend 1 on the Hint button to reveal one random missing letter.

What letters should I guess first in Hangman?

Start with vowels — especially E, A and O — then move through the most common consonants: T, N, S, R, H, D and L. Leave rare letters like J, Q, X and Z for last.

What are the hardest words to guess in Hangman?

Short words with rare letters (like jazz or jinx) and words without the usual vowels (like rhythm, psych or synth) are the toughest. The words in this game are everyday vocabulary, so they're more forgiving.

Can I play Hangman with friends?

Not at the moment — this version is a single-player game where you play against the word. You can always take turns with a friend on the same device and compare who solves more words.

Does Hangman help with spelling and vocabulary?

Yes — guessing letters one at a time builds spelling awareness, and playing by topic exposes you to new everyday words, which makes it a nice game for kids learning to read and spell.

Do I need to download Hangman?

No — there's nothing to download or install. This online Hangman game runs directly in your web browser, so you can start playing the moment the page loads.

Can I play Hangman on PC?

Yes. It works in any modern desktop browser on Windows, Mac, Linux and Chromebook, and you can guess letters by typing on your keyboard. It also works on tablets and phones.

How is this different from the Hangman board game, magnetic set, or toy?

The rules are the same classic idea — guess letters, and wrong guesses add pieces to the stick figure. The difference is that everything happens on screen: the game picks the word for you by topic, keeps score, and lets you earn diamonds for hints, so you don't need a second player to think of a word. This version is a bright 2D game with built-in topic words; you can't add your own words yet.

Is my progress saved?

Yes — your level, score, and diamonds are saved locally in your browser, so they're still there next time you visit on the same device and browser.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes — the on-screen keyboard is built for touch, and the layout switches to a stacked view on narrow screens.

Is Hangman suitable for kids?

The words are all everyday, kid-friendly vocabulary, and the stick figure is never hurt — he only gets sad if you run out of guesses. It's a fun way to practice spelling.
