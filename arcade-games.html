Free arcade games you can play right now

Arcade games are built around one idea: pick up the controls in a few seconds, then spend as long as you like getting better. Every arcade game on PlayItNow runs directly in your browser — no download, no account — and works with a keyboard or mouse on a computer and with touch on a phone or tablet. Whether you want a quick two-minute break or a chase for a higher score, here's what each one offers.

  • Neon Slither

    A Slither.io-style snake game. Grow your snake, dodge other snakes' trails, and climb the rank. Best for players who like steady, careful growth with a little risk-taking.

  • Neon Blob

    An Agar.io-style blob-eating game. Eat cells and rivals, grow bigger, and climb the rank. Best if you enjoy deciding when to chase and when to run.

  • Neon Onslaught

    A Vampire Survivors-style bullet-heaven game. Your weapon fires automatically at enemies while you focus on movement, level up, and survive as long as you can.

  • Neon Stack

    A one-tap tower game. Tap to drop each moving block with perfect timing, build the tallest tower you can, and beat your high score. Quick to learn and hard to put down.

  • Neon Dino

    A Dino-style endless runner. Jump over cacti, duck under flying obstacles, and run as far as you can as the pace picks up. A good pick when you only have a minute.

  • Neon Climb

    A vertical climbing game. Steer between platforms, avoid falling, and see how high you can get. Best for players who like short, repeatable runs.

How to pick an arcade game

  • Want something instant? Neon Dino and Neon Stack take one input and reward timing.
  • Prefer strategy under pressure? Neon Slither and Neon Blob are about growing safely while others hunt you.
  • Like surviving against odds? Neon Onslaught rewards steady movement over fast reflexes.
  • Want a steady climb? Neon Climb is all about controlled movement between platforms.

Tips for getting better at arcade games

  • Play a few short rounds first. The first run is for learning how the controls feel, not for scoring.
  • Stay calm when the pace picks up. Most arcade deaths come from over-correcting, not from being too slow.
  • Learn one pattern at a time. Notice how a single obstacle, enemy, or block behaves before worrying about the whole screen.
  • Take breaks. A fresh mind beats a tired one — a quick pause often improves your next run.
