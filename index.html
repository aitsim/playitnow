Your go-to spot for quick, free browser games

PlayItNow is a small, hand-built library of free online games — arcade action, puzzles, word games, a coloring book, and shop-management games with illustrated characters. There's nothing to download and no account to make: pick a game and start playing. Everything runs directly in your browser, so it works the same on a laptop, a phone, a tablet, or a school Chromebook. New here? Try Word Connect for a relaxing letter puzzle, Neon Dino for a one-button runner, or Buzzy Bear's Honey Shop if you like gathering, stocking, and selling.

What makes a game "browser-based"?

A browser game runs entirely inside your web browser using standard web technology, with nothing to install and nothing to keep updated. You open a link, and you're playing within seconds. That's the whole idea behind this site — every game here is built to load fast and play instantly, on any device that has a browser.

No. Every game runs directly in your browser. Just click a game and start playing.
