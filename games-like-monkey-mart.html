What people mean by "games like Monkey Mart"

Monkey Mart is a popular browser game where you run a little store: you gather goods, stock the shelves, serve customers, and spend your earnings on upgrades and expansions. It's a separate game by a different developer, and PlayItNow isn't affiliated with it — but the genre it belongs to is easy to describe, and if you enjoy it, you'll likely enjoy these two originals.

The common ingredients of a Monkey Mart-style game are:

  • Everything happens by walking. No menus for the core loop — you move your character near things and they happen.
  • A gather-stock-sell loop. Collect a resource, carry it to a shelf or counter, and let customers buy it.
  • Visible carrying. What you're holding stacks up on your character, so you can see it at a glance.
  • Coins you have to pick up. Money drops near the register instead of appearing in your total automatically.
  • Upgrades and expansion. Spend coins on speed, capacity, and new production areas that visibly grow your shop.

Getting started in a shop game

  • Fill your basket before you walk. One full trip beats several half-empty ones.
  • Keep the counter stocked. Customers only wait a few seconds; an empty counter is a missed sale.
  • Collect your coins. They wait where they drop until you walk over them.
  • Buy capacity early. A bigger basket pays for itself quickly by cutting down on walking.
  • Save for the expansion. The second hive and second fishing hole cost more than the small upgrades, but they open a whole new production spot.
  • Watch the hazards. Bees and seagulls only cost you a carried item, so approach from the side they've just left.

Games like Monkey Mart FAQ

Are these games free and playable without downloading?

Yes — both run in your browser with no download and no account, and your progress is saved locally on your device.

Do they work on mobile?

Yes. An on-screen directional pad appears automatically on touch devices, and on a computer you use WASD or the arrow keys.

Which one should I start with?

If you like a clear goal each round, start with Buzzy Bear's Honey Shop and its 90-second days. If you'd rather play at your own pace with no timer, try Penguin Fish Market.

Is this the same as Monkey Mart?

No. Monkey Mart is a separate game by a different developer. These are original PlayItNow games with their own characters, art, and rules that follow the same kind of walk-and-collect gameplay.
